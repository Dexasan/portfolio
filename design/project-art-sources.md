Prompts:

## ditchPoster

Edit the supplied actual DITCH logo into a restrained poster-print version. Preserve the exact distinctive D silhouette, integrated shapes, glitches and original magenta pink / pale blush / black identity. Reduce gradients to 4 or 5 flat ink levels with subtle screenprint grain. Do not redesign the logo, add text, add symbols or turn it blue. Crop away the large empty canvas so the existing mark is centered and fills about 85 percent of the image. Genuine transparent background.

## yappiePoster

Poster-print treatment of this actual Yappie mouth logo. Preserve the exact mouth silhouette, chalk teeth, open-mouth black negative space, vermilion red colour and print texture. Very gently simplify shading into 3 or 4 flat ink levels. Do not redraw, distort, add text, recolour blue or add decoration. Keep the whole mark centered with a little breathing room and a genuinely transparent background.

## roadPoster

Posterize this actual Road Rash PC cover into a clean limited-ink screenprint for a personal nostalgic game tribute. Preserve the two actual racing motorcycles, helmets, perspective, composition and original ROAD RASH wordmark exactly. Retain original yellow, red, white, purple and deep black colours; do not make it blue. Flatten photographic gradients to 6 or 7 bold ink levels with subtle coarse print grain, retaining recognizable detail and sharp legible original title. Remove only the bottom CD-ROM CLASSICS distribution banner by cropping below the main Road Rash logo. No new lettering, symbols or invented scene.

## roadRider

Extract the actual RED motorcycle and its helmeted rider on the RIGHT of this Road Rash cover as a compact transparent cutout. Preserve the original bike, helmet stripes, pose, camera angle and red, white, black colours. Remove the yellow bike, background, road, logos and lettering. Give this extracted original figure a bold 5-colour posterized screenprint treatment with strong clean edges that reads at small size. Include the entire front wheel and helmet. Preserve recognizable real cover artwork rather than inventing a new motorcycle. Genuine transparent background and tight centered crop.
